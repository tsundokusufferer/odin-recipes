Chicken Adobo

Home

Description

A classic Filipino dish, its' variations are almost countless. Practically every Filipino family has their own way of making it. Here, we have a sweet and sour chicken adobo variant adapted from Killer Chicken Adobo by Vanjo Merano of Panlasang Pinoy. Note that the portions listed below are for 4 people.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 lbs. chicken cut into serving pieces
  • 1 piece Knorr Chicken cube
  • 2 cups lemon lime soda
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup white vinegar
  • 5 pieces dried bay leaves
  • 1 head garlic
  • 2 teaspoons whole peppercorn
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil

Steps

  1. Submerge the chicken in a blend of soy sauce and 1 cup of lemon-lime soda, ensuring it is well-coated, then let it sit to marinate for a minimum of 30 minutes.
  2. Warm oil in a skillet, sear the marinated chicken pieces for 1 minute on each side, then pull them out of the pan and reserve them.
  3. In the remaining pan drippings, fry the garlic until golden brown. Return the seared chicken to the skillet alongside the reserved marinade, extra lemon-lime soda, whole peppercorns, and dried bay leaves, and bring the liquid to a rolling boil.
  4. Splash in the vinegar, give the contents a stir, and return the mixture to a boil.
  5. Drop in a Knorr chicken cube, put a lid on the pan, drop the heat to medium-low, and let it simmer for 20 minutes.
  6. Uncover the skillet, turn the burner up to medium, and let the liquid reduce completely while stirring occasionally.
  7. Plate, serve, and enjoy!

Recipe adapted from Killer Chicken Adobo by Vanjo Merano of Panlasang Pinoy.

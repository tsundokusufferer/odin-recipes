Chicken Adobo

Home

Description

A classic Filipino dish, its' variations are almost countless. Practically every Filipino family has their own way of making it. Here, we have a sweet and sour chicken adobo variant adapted from Killer Chicken Adobo by Vanjo Merano of Panlasang Pinoy. Note that the portions listed below are for 4 people.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 lbs. chicken cut into serving pieces
  • 1 piece Knorr Chicken cube
  • 2 cups lemon lime soda
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup white vinegar
  • 5 pieces dried bay leaves
  • 1 head garlic
  • 2 teaspoons whole peppercorn
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil

Steps

  1. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and 1 cup of lemon lime soda, then mix it. Afterwards, marinate it for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and proceed to pan-fry the marinated chicken for 1 minute for each side. Afterwards, remove it from the pan and set it aside.
  3. Using the leftover oil, sauté the garlic until it browns. Afterwards, put the pan-fried chicken back into the pan and add the remaining marinade, lemon lime soda, whole peppercorn, and dried bay leaves. Let it boil.
  4. Pour in the vinegar, then let the mixture boil. Afterwards, stir it.
  5. Add a Knorr Chicken Cube, then cover and reduce the heat between low to medium. Afterwards, cook it for 20 minutes.
  6. Remove the cover, then adjust the heat to medium. Afterwards, continue cooking, while stirring every few minutes until the sauce disappears.
  7. Serve and enjoy!

Recipe adapted from Killer Chicken Adobo by Vanjo Merano of Panlasang Pinoy.

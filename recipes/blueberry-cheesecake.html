Blueberry Cheesecake

Home

Description

A classic confectionery! It has a soft and creamy base, topped with a sweet glaze or reduction. Best served cold. Here, we have a recipe adapted from Blueberry Cheesecake by JJOHN32 on Allrecipes. Note that the portions listed below are for 12 people.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/4 cup melted butter
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar
  • 2 (8 ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 3/4 cup white sugar
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 cups frozen blueberries, dry pack
  • 1/3 cup blueberry jelly

Steps

  1. Start by bringing your oven up to a temperature of 325°F (165°C).
  2. In a mixing container, thoroughly blend together the graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and a couple of tablespoons of sugar. Firmly press this crumb mixture down to coat the base of a 9-inch springform pan.
  3. Work the cream cheese in a bowl until it achieves a smooth, soft texture. Slowly beat in the sour cream, 3/4 cup of sugar, flour, and vanilla extract. Incorporate the eggs individually, beating well after each addition. Transfer the resulting filling directly over your prepared crumb crust.
  4. Place the pan into the warmed oven and bake for roughly 1 hour, or until the center feels completely set and firm to the touch.
  5. Transfer the finished cheesecake to the refrigerator and let it chill completely for approximately 4 hours.
  6. Run a knife along the inside perimeter of the pan to free the sides, release the springform latch, and move the dessert onto a display platter. Scatter frozen blueberries across the surface. Gently warm jelly in a pan on low heat until liquefied, then drizzle it over the berries to create a glossy finish. Return the dessert to the fridge until it is time to serve.
  7. Plate, serve, and enjoy!

Recipe adapted from Blueberry Cheesecake by JJOHN32 on Allrecipes.

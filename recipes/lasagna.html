Description

Lasagna! One could describe it as pasta in the form of a cake. A thick slice of cheesy and meaty goodness, I've loved it since I was a child. Here, we have a recipe adapted from Easy Lasagna by Joanie Heger on Allrecipes. Note that the portions listed below are for 12 people.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 (32 ounce) jar spaghetti sauce
  • 32 ounces cottage cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 teaspoons dried parsley
  • Salt to taste
  • Ground black pepper to taste
  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1/2 cup water

Steps

  1. Before starting, bring all components to hand and preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet set over medium-high heat, breaking it apart continuously until fully cooked through (about 8 to 10 minutes). Drain away any rendered fat, pour in your spaghetti sauce, and let the blend simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. In a roomy bowl, mix together the cottage cheese, eggs, dried parsley, seasoning (salt and pepper), half of the grated Parmesan, and 2 cups of the mozzarella until uniform.
  4. Layer the baking dish (9x13 inches) by first coating the bottom with 3/4 cup of sauce. Lay down 3 dry lasagna noodles, top with 1 and 3/4 cups of the cheese filling, and add another 1/4 cup of sauce. Repeat this stacking process one more time, then finish the top with the final 3 noodles, remaining sauce, and the rest of the mozzarella and Parmesan. Trickle 1/2 cup of water around the inner perimeter of the pan and wrap the dish tightly in foil.
  5. Bake inside the warmed oven for 45 minutes. Strip away the foil and bake for an additional 10 minutes. Rest the dish for 10 minutes prior to slicing and dishing out.
  6. Plate, serve, and enjoy!

Recipe adapted from Easy Lasagna by Joanie Heger on Allrecipes.
